# Homepage Hero Rewrite Options

## Current Hero

**H1:** "Skip the 3-week wait. Text a clinician now."
**Subheadline:** "Full-service primary care — sick visits, chronic care, prescriptions, labs — for $99/month. No appointments. No hold music."
**CTA:** "Start Membership - $99/mo"
**Meta title:** "Text-First Primary Care | Present Health"

### What's working
- The H1 is punchy and addresses a real pain point (3-week wait)
- Price is visible in the CTA
- "No appointments. No hold music." is memorable
- Badge says "Accepting New Members" (urgency)

### What's missing
- No social proof (no reviews, no member count, no rating)
- No physician face or credentials above the fold
- No state availability confirmation
- Meta title doesn't contain high-volume keywords ("virtual primary care," "$99")
- "Text a clinician" is accurate but "text your doctor" is what patients search for
- No HSA mention above the fold (biggest 2026 differentiator)

---

## Recommended Rewrite — Option A (Conversion-focused)

**Badge:** "Now HSA-eligible | Accepting new members in 15 states"

**H1:** "Text your doctor. Get real answers. $99/month."

**Subheadline:** "Full-service primary care for adults 18+ — sick visits, chronic care, prescriptions, labs, and more. Messaging-first. Board-certified physician-led. No insurance required."

**CTA Primary:** "Start Membership — $99/mo"
**CTA Secondary:** "Or try a single visit — $99"

**Trust pills (below CTA):**
- Board-certified physician-led
- Licensed in 15 states
- HSA-eligible in 2026
- HIPAA compliant

**Social proof (below trust pills):**
- Physician headshot + "Your care team is led by Dr. [Name], MD — Board-Certified Family Medicine"
- "Rated [X]/5 by members" (add when you have 5+ reviews)

**Meta title:** "Virtual Primary Care — $99/Month, No Insurance Needed | Present Health"

**Meta description:** "Text your doctor for sick visits, chronic care, prescriptions, and labs. $99/month, all included. Board-certified physician-led. HSA-eligible. Licensed in 15 states."

### Why this works better for patient acquisition

1. **"Text your doctor"** matches the exact phrase patients search for and is warmer than "text a clinician"
2. **"$99/month" in H1** — price transparency in the headline eliminates the #1 question visitors have and increases qualified traffic (people who click already know the price)
3. **"No insurance required"** in subheadline — proactively addresses the #1 confusion point
4. **"Board-certified physician-led"** — differentiates from NP-only and AI-first competitors (K Health, nurse practitioner telehealth services)
5. **HSA badge** — captures the 2026 wave; patients with HDHPs see this and think "I can use my HSA for this"
6. **Physician face above fold** — healthcare is personal; showing the doctor converts 20-40% better than faceless tech branding
7. **Meta title with "Virtual Primary Care"** — targets 12,100 monthly searches vs. "Text-First Primary Care" (0 search volume as a phrase)

## State Confirmation Widget (add below hero)

```
[Your state] [dropdown: AZ, FL, IN, KY, MI, MN, NC, NE, NH, OH, RI, TX, UT, WA, WI]

[Check Availability]

--> "Yes! Present Health is available in [State]. Start your membership today."
--> "We're not in [State] yet. Join the waitlist and we'll notify you."
```

### Why this matters

The #1 reason virtual healthcare visitors bounce is uncertainty about geographic availability. A state selector above the fold eliminates that friction within 3 seconds. Patients who confirm availability are 5x more likely to convert than those who have to hunt for it.
