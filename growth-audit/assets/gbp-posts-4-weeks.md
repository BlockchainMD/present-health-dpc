## Posting Guidelines

1. **Frequency:** 1 post per week minimum. Google rewards consistent posting activity.
2. **Images:** Always include an image. Posts without images get 50% fewer views. Use physician photos, branded graphics, or illustrative mockups. Never use identifiable patient photos.
3. **Length:** 150-300 words per post. Front-load the most important information in the first 2 lines (Google truncates after ~100 characters in mobile previews).
4. **CTA button:** Always include one. Use "Learn more," "Sign up," or "Book online" button types available in GBP.
5. **Compliance:** Never make specific medical claims. Never reference specific patient outcomes. Never use terms like "cure," "guarantee," "best," or "24/7."
6. **Expiration:** GBP posts expire after 6 months. After 8 weeks, start recycling with fresh copy and updated data points.
