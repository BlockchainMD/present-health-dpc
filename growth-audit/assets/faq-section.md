# Optimized FAQ Section

## SEO-targeted FAQ for Homepage and /pricing

These questions are written to match real "People Also Ask" and search queries. Each answer is structured for featured snippet extraction (direct answer first, then detail).

### Is Present Health real primary care?

Yes. Present Health provides the same scope of primary care as a traditional doctor's office — sick visits, chronic condition management, medications, lab orders, wellness planning, and care coordination. The difference is delivery: care happens through secure messaging first, with video visits when clinically appropriate. About 80-90% of primary care needs can be handled without an in-person exam. Your care team is led by a board-certified family medicine physician.

### How much does Present Health cost?

$99 per month for unlimited primary care, or $990 per year (save $198). There are no per-visit fees, no copays, and no surprise bills. If you prefer, a single visit is available for $99 with no membership required. Employer plans start at $29 per employee per month.

### Can I use my HSA or FSA to pay for Present Health?

Yes. Direct Primary Care (DPC) memberships became HSA-eligible in 2026. Present Health's $99/month fee is within the IRS limit of $150/month for DPC. You can pay for your membership directly from your Health Savings Account. Consult a tax professional for guidance specific to your situation.

### Do you take insurance?

No. Present Health operates outside the insurance system entirely. This means no copays, no prior authorizations, no claim denials, and no surprise bills. You pay one transparent monthly fee. We recommend keeping health insurance for emergencies, hospitalizations, and specialist visits.

### How fast will I hear back from my doctor?

Within 4 business hours during practice hours (Monday through Friday, 8am-8pm ET). Messages sent outside business hours receive a next-business-morning response. Most members hear back faster — but 4 hours is the commitment.

### What states is Present Health available in?

Present Health is currently licensed in 15 states: Arizona, Florida, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Minnesota, North Carolina, Nebraska, New Hampshire, Ohio, Rhode Island, Texas, Utah, Washington, and Wisconsin. Availability depends on where you are located at the time of your visit. We are actively expanding to additional states.

### What can Present Health help with?

Acute care (UTIs, respiratory illness, rashes, allergies, minor injuries, ear and eye concerns), chronic condition management (diabetes, hypertension, thyroid, cholesterol), prescription management and refills, lab ordering and interpretation, annual wellness planning, mental health screening and medication, medication reviews, specialist referrals and coordination, and care navigation and triage.

### What can Present Health NOT help with?

Emergency care (call 911), conditions requiring a hands-on physical examination, in-person procedures (stitches, biopsies, pap smears), specialist visits (we refer and coordinate), and hospital care. If something needs in-person attention, your care team will tell you and help you find the right provider.

### Is Present Health the same as concierge medicine?

No. Concierge medicine typically costs $2,000-$10,000 per year and often still bills insurance on top of the retainer fee. Direct Primary Care (DPC) like Present Health charges one flat monthly fee ($99/month) with no insurance billing and no per-visit charges. DPC is designed to make personalized primary care affordable, not exclusive.

### Is my health information private and secure?

Yes. All messaging and health data is protected by HIPAA-compliant encryption. Present Health uses bank-grade security for all communications. Your health information is never sold or shared with third parties.

### Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Cancel anytime with no cancellation fee and no contract. Monthly members can cancel before their next billing date. Annual members can cancel and will retain access through the end of their paid year.

### What if I need to see a doctor in person?

If your care team determines that an in-person exam, procedure, or imaging is medically necessary, they will tell you directly, recommend the appropriate type of provider (urgent care, specialist, ER), and help coordinate the plan before and after your visit. Present Health handles the triage and follow-up so you are not navigating the system alone.

### How is Present Health different from other telehealth services?

Most telehealth services (PlushCare, Doctor on Demand, Teladoc) charge per visit, bill insurance, and connect you with a different doctor each time. Present Health is a Direct Primary Care practice — you have a dedicated care team, communicate through messaging (not appointments), pay one monthly fee with no per-visit charges, and build an ongoing relationship with clinicians who know your history.
