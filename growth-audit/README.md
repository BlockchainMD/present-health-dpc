## 1. Local Search Visibility Audit

### Current State

Present Health has **zero visibility** in local search. No Google Business Profile listing exists. The website does not rank for any high-intent local or DPC keywords.

### Key Findings

| Finding | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| No GBP listing | Invisible in Google Maps, knowledge panel, and local pack for all searches |
| Homepage meta title ("Text-First Primary Care") contains zero high-volume search terms | Missing 12,100/mo "virtual primary care" and 22,000/mo "primary care without insurance" searches |
| State pages exist but are not optimized for "[DPC/telehealth] + [state]" queries | HSA for America's state guides currently dominate these SERPs |
| No DPC directory listings (DPC Alliance, DPC Frontier, FindMyDirectDoctor) | Missing referral traffic from patients actively searching DPC directories |
| No comparison content (vs. PlushCare, One Medical, concierge medicine) | Missing high-intent mid-funnel comparison searches |
| No HSA + DPC educational content | Missing the single biggest DPC tailwind in 2026 (4,400/mo search volume and growing) |

### Search Landscape

- **"Direct primary care near me"** (14,800/mo): Local pack results dominate. Only brick-and-mortar DPC practices appear. No virtual DPC ranks.
- **"Primary care without insurance"** (22,000/mo): Editorial content dominates (GoodRx, AARP, KFF Health News). No DPC practice ranks organically.
- **"Virtual primary care"** (12,100/mo): Amazon One Medical and health system portals dominate. No DPC model appears.
- **"HSA direct primary care 2026"** (4,400/mo): HSA for America guides dominate. Massive opportunity for Present Health at $99/mo (under $150 IRS limit).

**Bottom line:** There is no virtual-only DPC practice ranking for any high-intent primary care keyword. This is the gap.

## 2. Competitor Analysis

### Direct Competitive Threats

| Competitor | Threat Level | Why |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| Amazon One Medical | HIGH | Brand recognition, $99/yr with Prime (misleading but effective), physical locations, paid search dominance |
| Sesame Care | MEDIUM | Strong reviews (4.8/5), transparent pricing, $37+/visit marketplace model |
| K Health | LOW | AI-first with poor trust signals (BBB F rating), $49/mo |
| PlushCare | MEDIUM | 50-state coverage, $19.99/mo entry, insurance billing (different model but captures same searches) |
| Local DPC practices | LOW per-practice, HIGH collectively | Dominate local pack in each state. Individual practices are small but collectively own "DPC near me" |

### Key Competitive Advantages to Exploit

1. **Price-model clarity:** Most competitors have hidden per-visit fees. Present Health's $99/mo is truly all-inclusive. This should be the central message.
2. **HSA eligibility:** Present Health at $99/mo is under the $150/mo IRS limit. Amazon One Medical's $99/yr model doesn't qualify as DPC for HSA purposes.
3. **Physician-led, not AI-first:** K Health is AI with human backup. Present Health is physician-led with technology enabling access. Trust matters in healthcare.
4. **Continuity of care:** Sesame Care is a marketplace (different doctor each time). PlushCare is appointment-based. Present Health offers a dedicated care team relationship.
5. **Two major competitors are dead:** Forward Health shut down (Nov 2024). Heal was acquired and pivoted to Medicare. The market is consolidating toward sustainable models.

## 3. GBP Audit

### Current State: No listing exists.

This is the single highest-priority fix. Every recommendation below depends on having a GBP listing.

### Setup Recommendations

- **Listing type:** Service Area Business (SAB) — do not display a physical address
- **Primary category:** Family practice physician
- **Secondary categories:** Doctor, Medical clinic, General practitioner
- **Service areas:** All 15 licensed states
- **Enable:** "Online care" and "Offers telehealth" attributes
- **Booking link:** presenthealthmd.com/join
- **Description:** See `assets/gbp-description.md`
- **Services:** See `assets/gbp-service-list.md`

### Virtual Practice Limitation

Google's local pack heavily favors physical locations. A SAB listing will appear in knowledge panel for branded searches and some broad searches, but will NOT appear in the local 3-pack for "doctor near me" queries. If Present Health ever establishes any physical presence (even a virtual office address) in a key market (Dallas, Miami, Columbus), a location-based listing would unlock local pack visibility in that market.

Full GBP recommendations: `spreadsheet/03-gbp-fixes.tsv`

## 4. Website Conversion Audit

### What's Working

- Homepage hero is clear and punchy ("Skip the 3-week wait. Text a clinician now.")
- Price is transparent ($99/mo visible in CTA)
- Scenario cards demonstrate real use cases effectively
- FAQ covers core objections
- State pages exist for all 15 states
- Cost comparison calculator exists on pricing page
- Structured data (schema) is well-implemented

### Critical Gaps

| Gap | Impact on Conversion | Fix Effort |
|-----|---------------------|------------|
| **No social proof above fold** — no reviews, no member count, no rating | Patients seeing zero social proof will choose a competitor with reviews | Easy (add badge once 5+ reviews exist) |
| **No physician face above fold** — "Meet Our Physician" is a link, not a presence | Healthcare is a trust purchase. No face = no trust. Competitors show their doctors prominently. | Easy (add headshot + credentials) |
| **No state confirmation above fold** — patients must scroll to find if they're eligible | #1 bounce reason for virtual care: "Is this available where I live?" | Medium (add state dropdown widget) |
| **Meta title missing key search terms** — "Text-First Primary Care" has 0 search volume | Missing 12,100/mo "virtual primary care" clicks | Easy (change meta title) |
| **HSA eligibility is whispered, not shouted** — small text below pricing cards | The biggest DPC tailwind in 2026 is buried. Every HDHP holder should see this immediately. | Easy (add HSA badge/section) |
| **Join page has two equal-weight CTAs** — membership vs. single visit 50/50 | Decision paralysis. Membership is the business. Single visit should be de-emphasized. | Easy (redesign CTA hierarchy) |
| **No comparison table** — "How we're different" section doesn't name competitors or show data | Patients comparing options need a side-by-side. Without it, they leave to research elsewhere. | Medium (add comparison section) |

Full conversion fix list: `spreadsheet/04-website-conversion-fixes.tsv`
