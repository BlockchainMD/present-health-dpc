## Primary Care Services

### Virtual Primary Care Visit
Secure messaging and video visits with your dedicated care team. No appointments needed. Response within 4 business hours, Monday through Friday.

### Chronic Disease Management
Ongoing management of diabetes, hypertension, thyroid conditions, high cholesterol, and other chronic conditions. Medication adjustments, lab monitoring, and care planning — all through messaging.

### Prescription Management
New prescriptions, refills, and medication reviews. Your clinician sends prescriptions to your preferred pharmacy. No per-visit fee for prescription requests.

### Lab Ordering and Interpretation
Lab orders sent to local Quest or Labcorp locations. Results reviewed and explained by your care team through secure messaging. Lab draw costs are separate.

### Annual Wellness Planning
Personalized prevention and wellness plans based on your age, health history, and goals. Includes screening recommendations and health risk assessment.

### Mental Health Screening and Medication
Screening for depression, anxiety, and other common mental health conditions. Medication management when appropriate. Referrals to specialists when needed.

### Medication Review
Comprehensive review of all current medications for interactions, side effects, and optimization. Coordination with specialists on medication changes.

### Specialist Referral Coordination
When specialist care is needed, your care team identifies the right specialist, sends referrals, and coordinates before and after the visit.

### Urgent Sick Visits
Same-day messaging for UTIs, respiratory illness, rashes, allergies, minor injuries, ear and eye concerns. No appointment needed — just message your care team.

### Care Navigation and Triage
Not sure if you need urgent care, the ER, or can wait? Your care team helps you decide the right level of care and coordinates next steps.
