# HIPAA-Safe Review Response Templates

## Critical Rules

1. **NEVER acknowledge that the reviewer is a patient.** Even if they say they are. Your response should be written as if you're responding to anyone who left a review.
2. **NEVER reference diagnoses, symptoms, medications, treatments, visit details, or any health information** — even if the reviewer shared it publicly.
3. **NEVER say "we're glad your [condition] improved" or "sorry about your [symptom]."** This confirms PHI.
4. **Always respond within 24 hours.** Response time signals active management to Google and prospective patients.
5. **Keep responses genuine and specific to the sentiment, not the medical details.**

---

## 5-Star Review Responses

### Template 5A — General Positive

Thank you for taking the time to share this, {reviewer_name}. We're glad the experience has been positive. Our team works hard to make healthcare feel less frustrating, and hearing that it's working means a lot. We're here whenever you need us.

### Template 5B — Mentions Access/Speed

{reviewer_name}, thank you for this. Access to care without unnecessary barriers is exactly what we're trying to build. We appreciate you sharing your experience — it helps others understand what this kind of care looks like.

### Template 5C — Mentions the Care Team

That means a lot, {reviewer_name}. Our care team genuinely cares about every person they work with, and feedback like yours reinforces why they do this work. Thank you for the kind words.

### Template 5D — Short and Warm

Thank you, {reviewer_name}. We appreciate you and are glad to be part of your health journey.

## 4-Star Review Responses

### Template 4A — Positive with Minor Reservation

Thank you for the thoughtful feedback, {reviewer_name}. We're glad the overall experience has been good, and we take your input seriously. If there's anything specific we can improve, our team is always reachable. We appreciate you.

### Template 4B — Constructive

{reviewer_name}, we appreciate both the kind words and the honest feedback. We're always working to improve, and input from the people we serve is the best way to get there. Don't hesitate to reach out directly if there's anything we can do better.

---

## 3-Star Review Responses

### Template 3A — Balanced

{reviewer_name}, thank you for sharing your experience honestly. We take feedback like this seriously because it helps us improve. If you'd be open to it, we'd welcome the chance to hear more about what we could do better. You can reach us at {contact_email} anytime.

### Template 3B — Acknowledging Room for Improvement

We appreciate your candor, {reviewer_name}. A mixed experience isn't what we aim for, and we'd like to understand how we can do better for you. Please feel free to reach out to us directly at {contact_email} — we want to make this right.

---

## 2-Star Review Responses

### Template 2A — Empathetic, Non-Defensive

{reviewer_name}, we're sorry the experience didn't meet your expectations. We hold ourselves to a high standard and it's clear we fell short here. We'd sincerely like the opportunity to understand what happened and how we can improve. Please reach out to us at {contact_email} when you have a moment — we'll make it a priority.

### Template 2B — Direct

Thank you for the honest feedback, {reviewer_name}. This isn't the experience we want anyone to have. We'd like to learn more and see if there's a way to make things better. Our team is available at {contact_email} and we'll respond promptly.

---

## 1-Star Review Responses

### Template 1A — Compassionate, No Defensiveness

{reviewer_name}, we're genuinely sorry to hear this. Every person's experience matters to us, and we take feedback like this to heart. We'd welcome the chance to speak with you directly and understand how we can do better. Please reach out to us at {contact_email} — we want to listen.

### Template 1B — Brief, Professional

We appreciate you sharing this, {reviewer_name}, and we're sorry the experience was poor. We'd like to make it right. Please contact us at {contact_email} so we can follow up personally.

---

## Fake/Spam Review Response

### Template Spam

We don't have a record of this interaction and believe this review may have been posted in error. If you've had a genuine experience with Present Health, we'd welcome the chance to discuss it. Please reach out to {contact_email}.

**Then:** Flag the review in GBP as "fake/spam" through the reporting tool.

## What NOT to Do — HIPAA Examples

| Reviewer Says | WRONG Response | RIGHT Response |
|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| "Dr. Smith diagnosed my thyroid issue so fast" | "We're glad Dr. Smith was able to help with your thyroid." | "Thank you for the kind words about our team. We're glad the experience was positive." |
| "They helped me manage my diabetes medication" | "Happy to help with your diabetes management." | "We're glad you've found the care helpful. Our team is always here for you." |
| "The prescription they sent worked great" | "Glad the prescription resolved things for you." | "Thank you for sharing this. We appreciate your trust in our care team." |
| "They misdiagnosed me" | "We apologize for the misdiagnosis." | "We're sorry the experience didn't meet your expectations. Please reach out to us at {contact_email} so we can understand what happened." |

**The golden rule:** Respond to the *sentiment*, never the *medical content*.
